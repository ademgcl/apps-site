Privacy Policy

Crowd Clear keeps your data local.

Effective date: June 22, 2026

Data Collection

Crowd Clear does not collect personal data from players.

Analytics and Tracking

Crowd Clear does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Accounts

Crowd Clear does not require account creation, cloud login, or sign-in to play levels, retry runs, or track best scores.

Local Save Data

The game stores progression such as best times, best chain, and per-level stars locally on the device so the single-player experience works offline.

Children

Crowd Clear does not target children and does not collect any data from any user, including children under 13.
