Support for Crowd Clear.

Crowd Clear is a single-player routing puzzle for iPhone and iPad, built around tap-to-flip switches, color-matching gates, and an endless procedurally-scaled station curve that gets harder every level.

Help Contact

For bugs, gameplay issues, or questions, email ademgcl@gmail.com.

What To Include

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The station number and mechanic (WEAVE, RUSH, MASTER, ...) where the issue occurred
  • A short description of what happened

Response Scope

Gameplay: switch routing, gate matching, chain bonuses, rush mode, and station progression.

Technical: launch problems, crashes, save data, and input issues.

Controls Quick Reference

Tap a switch to flip it and re-route the next train. Build chain bonuses by routing trains to their matching gates in a row. Endless mode begins at Station 03 and scales forever — every station number is the same level on every play.

Tips

  • Read the next gate pip before flipping — every switch has a colored target.
  • Wrong-color trains still arrive at the gate (no points, chain broken) so the level never stalls.
  • Chain x2+ for score multipliers, rush mode for bonus waves and big payouts.
