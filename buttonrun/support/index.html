One-Tap Timing Runner

Support for Button Run.

Button Run is a single-player arcade timing game for iPhone and iPad, built around a one-button phase mechanic, fast rooms, instant retries, and offline play.

Help Contact

For bugs, gameplay issues, or questions, email ademgcl@gmail.com.

What To Include

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The room, hazard, or medal goal where the issue occurred
  • A short description of what happened

Response Scope

Gameplay: phase timing, hazards, tokens, medals, and progression.

Technical: launch problems, crashes, save data, and input issues.

Controls Quick Reference

Tap to activate the phase button. Tap after a failure to retry the room, or tap after a clear to advance to the next room.
